# Reviews of the notes

One line per review of a document under `notes/`. It records what was
reviewed, at which commit, by whom and why, and where the issues it raised
are tracked. Reviews are dated by the day the review was carried out; the
file name carries that date. Issues raised by a review live on
[`notes/OPEN-ISSUES.md`](../OPEN-ISSUES.md) (calculation) or on a campaign
board (code), never in the review itself. Add a line when a review is
written; update its status column when its issues are all closed or when a
later review supersedes it.
