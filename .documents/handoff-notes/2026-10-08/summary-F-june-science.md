## 1. Dated timeline

### 21 June, 21:38 → 22 June 13:46 — "Sampling strategy for instanton parameter sensitivity analysis"

*Question.* Which of (δN★, N_init, N_final) controls (i) max of C or C̄, (ii) threshold crossing, (iii) collapse scale / M_PBH? David's stated prior: smallest scale fixed by N_final; largest scale larger by exp[(N_init−N_final)+δN★]; "the peak zeta is always achieved at the endpoint of the instanton ... and is always equal to delta_Nstar"; collapse scale can exceed the largest scale via the C̄ extrapolation branch. Compute budget: 200–300 instantons/min on an M1; the 25 000-instanton fiducial run gave 10 shards of ~550–620 MB each.

*Design decisions (all recorded).* Step-1 N_final-decoupling test; Sobol/LHC in (δN★, ΔN) with coarse N_final; GP regressor on the signed distance C̄_max − 0.4 rather than a classifier; scalars-only storage; Sobol indices on the surrogate. David rejected weighting-down extrapolated points: the extrapolation "is done using an exact analytic formula and should be reliable". David proposed the unified single-`RayWorkPool` pipeline (prompts 6–8) after noticing the two-pass architecture made `set_store_full_values(False)` unsafe. David set the integrity rule: a recompute whose scalars disagree with a stored row "is an integrity failure and shouldn't happen ... we would raise an exception" (`PIPELINE_SCALAR_INTEGRITY_RTOL = 1e-4`). Footer provenance `[summary-only]` added after David could not tell the two smoke-test runs apart. Output: prompts 01–10 and `handoff-sparse-sampling.md`.

### 22 June, 01:45 → 12:18 — "Gaussian process regression and classification explained"

David probed what a GP actually returns. Conclusions he drew: "you ONLY EVER get `mu*` and `sigma*` out"; GP regression on (r_PBH, M_PBH) "might work, but also might be useless/misleading". He proposed the ansatz `M_PBH = α N_final^a (N_init − N_final + δN★)^b`, caught the assistant's algebra slip (b ≈ e gives a product ΔN·δN★, not the sum), and then rejected his own ansatz: C_max "can fall anywhere between these smallest and largest scales, or even be larger ... It's not clear how to capture where C_max falls", and any learned r_max map "will plausibly depend on the potential function and not just the parameters". Agreed outcome: GP is a fixed-potential interpolator, not physics. **Not recorded** (the handoffs present the GP plan without this caveat).
